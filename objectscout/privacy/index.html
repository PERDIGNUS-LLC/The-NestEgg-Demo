ObjectScout Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

ObjectScout is operated by Perdignus LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information ObjectScout processes, why it is processed, how third-party services are involved, and the choices available to users.

1. Information ObjectScout Processes

Camera images and visual scans

ObjectScout allows users to photograph objects for identification, inventory organization, and appraisal features. Images submitted for AI analysis are transmitted securely to ObjectScout's backend and forwarded to Google Gemini, the third-party AI provider used to perform object recognition, classification, and appraisal.

ObjectScout's own backend does not intentionally store submitted images. Each image is relayed to Google Gemini for the purpose of generating a response and is not written to any persistent database or file storage operated by ObjectScout. Google processes the image according to its own data-handling policies, linked below.

Inventory information

As you catalog items, ObjectScout creates information such as item names, descriptions, assigned locations, tags, and appraisal results (for Sniper Mode).

This inventory data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to or retained on ObjectScout's backend. Photos and item details you save remain on your device unless you choose to share them yourself. When a photo is analyzed, only the image itself is transmitted transiently (as described above) — the resulting catalog entry you save is stored locally.

Installation and monetization identifiers

ObjectScout uses a pseudonymous installation identifier to maintain server-controlled functionality such as:

  • Snipe balances;
  • rewarded-ad reload progression;
  • reward verification;
  • transaction deduplication;
  • abuse prevention;
  • rate limiting; and
  • monetization-state persistence.

This identifier is a one-way hash derived from your device, not your name, email, or other directly identifying information. ObjectScout does not require a traditional user account for these functions.

Advertising information

ObjectScout uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from network information, ad-interaction information, consent choices, and similar information according to Google's own policies and applicable law.

In regions where consent is required, ObjectScout uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent before requesting ads.

Winston voice functionality

ObjectScout's assistant, Winston, can speak responses aloud. The text of Winston's response is sent to ElevenLabs solely to generate spoken audio; this text-to-speech request does not include a recording of your voice.

Separately, ObjectScout's voice-search feature ("Ask Winston") allows you to ask a spoken question about your inventory. When you use this feature, a recording of your spoken question is transmitted to Google Gemini to interpret the request. This is distinct from the text-to-speech function above and only occurs when you actively use voice search.

2. How Information Is Used

ObjectScout processes information as necessary to:

  • identify and organize objects;
  • provide inventory functionality;
  • perform Sniper appraisals;
  • generate Winston voice responses and interpret voice search requests;
  • verify rewarded-ad transactions;
  • maintain Snipe balances and monetization state;
  • prevent duplicate rewards, fraud, abuse, and excessive automated requests;
  • provide and measure advertising;
  • troubleshoot and maintain the service; and
  • improve application reliability and functionality.

3. Third-Party Services

ObjectScout's current production architecture uses the following third-party services:

Google Gemini

Used for AI-assisted image/object analysis, appraisal, and voice-search interpretation. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob / Google User Messaging Platform

Used for advertising, rewarded advertisements, ad measurement, and consent management. See Google's Advertising Policy.

ElevenLabs

Used for Winston text-to-speech generation. Only response text is sent — not audio recordings. See ElevenLabs' Privacy Policy.

Vercel

Used to operate ObjectScout's backend infrastructure, which relays AI requests and manages monetization state. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

Upstash Redis

Used to maintain server-side monetization state — Snipe balances, reward tickets, transaction deduplication, and rate-limit counters. This store does not contain inventory data or images.

Each service receives only the information necessary for its specific function, as described above — not a shared copy of all user information.

4. Advertising and Consent

ObjectScout uses Google AdMob to display advertising. For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other jurisdictions where required, ObjectScout presents a consent message before requesting ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform.

Not all advertising shown in ObjectScout is personalized — the type of ad served depends on your consent choices and applicable regional requirements. Where Google indicates that privacy options are required for your region, you may revisit your advertising consent choices at any time through ObjectScout's Privacy Options setting.

5. Data Storage and Security

ObjectScout uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect information transmitted between the app and its backend, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and ObjectScout cannot guarantee absolute security.

Information is handled differently depending on its type:

  • Inventory data (item names, descriptions, photos, appraisals) is stored locally on your device.
  • AI-processing requests (images, voice queries) are transmitted transiently to Gemini and are not retained by ObjectScout's backend.
  • Monetization state (Snipe balances, reward records) is stored server-side, tied to your pseudonymous installation identifier.
  • Advertising data is processed by Google and its partners according to their own policies.

6. Data Retention

Inventory data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. AI-processing requests (images and voice queries) are transient and are not intentionally retained by ObjectScout's backend after a response is generated. Server-side monetization state persists for as long as your installation identifier remains active, to maintain accurate Snipe balances and prevent abuse; ObjectScout does not currently enforce a fixed deletion schedule for this operational state.

7. Children's Privacy

ObjectScout is not directed to children under 13 and Perdignus LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If Perdignus LLC becomes aware that personal information from a child has been collected contrary to applicable law, reasonable steps will be taken to address it.

8. User Choices

  • Camera and microphone permissions can be controlled at any time through your device settings.
  • Winston's voice can be turned off entirely within ObjectScout's settings — this stops the related voice request from being made, not just local playback.
  • Where Google indicates privacy options are required for your region, you may revisit your advertising consent choices through ObjectScout's Privacy Options setting.
  • You may stop using ObjectScout and remove locally stored application data at any time by uninstalling or clearing app data, subject to server-side monetization state that may remain for legitimate operational purposes described above.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ObjectScout evolves. The Effective Date above will be updated whenever material revisions are made.
